# CAD & Mechanical Design Portfolio

Portfolio featuring 3D parametric models, mechanical assemblies, and manufacturing documentation developed in **SolidWorks** and **AutoCAD**.

---

## Projects

### 1. Laser-Cut Multi-Piece Assembly (Jewelry Box)
* **Software:** SolidWorks, AutoCAD
* **Manufacturing Process:** Laser Cutting (3mm sheets)
* **Key Features:** Interlocking press-fit joinery, geometric tolerance optimization, automated 2D nesting DXF exports, and detailed Bill of Materials (BOM).

---

### 2. Coca-Cola Bottle Blow/Injection Mold Design
* **Software:** SolidWorks
* **Manufacturing Process:** Mold Design / Tooling (Injection/Blow Molding)
* **Key Features:** Core and cavity split design, parting line definition, draft angle integration for clean demolding, and internal volume/shrinkage compensation.

---

### 3. Sheet & Packaging Design: Foldable Cardboard/Cartonite Tray
* **Software:** AutoCAD, SolidWorks (Sheet Metal / Packaging Layout)
* **Manufacturing Process:** Die-cutting and folding (Cartonite / Paperboard)
* **Key Features:** Flat-pattern development, score/crease line layout, structural interlocking tabs without adhesives, and material yield optimization.

---

### 4. Industrial Safety Device Enclosure (Adapted Design)
* **Software:** SolidWorks
* **Manufacturing Process:** FDM 3D Printing (PLA Matte / PLA Basic)
* **Key Features:** Customized redesign adapted from a base GrabCAD model, featuring bespoke gas sensor cutouts, modified component clearances, and snap-fit optimization for prototyping.
* **Base Reference:** Adapted from [GrabCAD Arduino UNO Case](https://grabcad.com/library/arduino-uno-case-14).
